Lo que hemos hecho

The present perfect connects a completed action or experience with the present: Hemos limpiado el río (we have cleaned the river). The result matters now. Use it to discuss experiences, changes, or actions within a time period connected to now; regional preferences vary.

Present tense of haber + past participle. Only haber changes with the subject.

El orden de las palabras

No hemos contaminado el lago. Nos hemos propuesto reciclar. ¿Has visto la catarata? Put no and object/reflexive pronouns before haber. Keep the auxiliary and participle together.

Watch out: Hemos protegido, never hemos protegidos. With haber, the participle does not agree with gender or number. Use he, not soy or tengo, as the auxiliary.

¿Qué has hecho este año?

Ask a partner about three outdoor experiences. Follow up with a detail question: ¿Has acampado en el bosque? ¿Dónde acampaste? A specific completed past event can use the preterite.

Raíces irregulares

poner → pondr- · tener → tendr- · salir → saldr- · haber → habr- · caber → cabr- · querer → querr- · decir → dir- · hacer → har-. Also poder → podr-; saber → sabr-; venir → vendr-. Add the ordinary future endings: tendremos, dirán, haré, podremos.

Dos maneras de hablar del futuro

Vamos a sembrar árboles means “we are going to plant trees”; sembraremos árboles means “we will plant trees.” The forms often overlap. Ir a + infinitive commonly presents a plan or a future event connected to the present.

La probabilidad en el presente

¿Quién será? Será la profesora. Here the future expresses a guess about now: “Who could it be? It is probably the professor.” Context distinguishes a prediction from a present-time guess.

Watch out: Keep the infinitive: reciclaré, not reciclé (I recycled). For impersonal haber, use singular habrá: Habrá más árboles. In a realistic si clause, use present + future: Si cuidamos la tierra, la tierra nos cuidará.

La comunidad del futuro

With a partner, make three predictions and one promise. Ask ¿Qué cambiará? ¿Qué haremos nosotros? ¿Cómo protegeremos el agua? Explain the reason for each idea.
