      Google Docs comments and useful feedback

      Open your partner’s outline in Google Docs. Highlight the relevant words or section, choose Add comment, write your feedback, and post it. Leave the outline text in your partner’s voice.

      • Thesis: “I understand your main claim as ___. How does ___ help the creator persuade this audience?”
      • Evidence: “This example seems useful because ___. Can you explain why this audience might respond to it?”
      • Organization or evaluation: “Could you develop ___ here? It would help me understand ___.”

      Include a specific observation and a useful question or next step. “Looks good” by itself does not give your partner enough to work with. Keep comments visible until you have used them.

      For three students, review in a circle: A reviews B, B reviews C, and C reviews A.
