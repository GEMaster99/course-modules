In-text citations: connect evidence to the source list

The name or title in your in-text citation must lead to the beginning of the matching References or Works Cited entry. Add a page, paragraph, or timestamp where your style needs one.

EvidenceAPAMLA
Book quotation, page 52(Rivera, 2023, p. 52)(Rivera 52)
Unpaginated webpage quotation(Rivera, 2025, para. 4)(Rivera)
YouTube advertisement at 0:24(Bright Harbor, 2025, 0:24)(Bright Harbor 00:24)
TEDx talk on YouTube at 2:10(TEDx Talks, 2025, 2:10)(Rivera 02:10)
Reprinted speech transcript(Patel, 2019/2024, para. 6)(Patel)
Full episode at 12:30(Rivera & Chen, 2024, 12:30)(“Five Stars” 00:12:30)

Do not repeat a name already supplied in your sentence. For example: APA Rivera (2023) argues… followed by (p. 52); MLA Rivera argues… followed by (52). Several works by the same author require additional details to distinguish the entries.

Missing an author, date, title, or page number?
  • Check whether an organization is responsible before concluding there is no author. If no author can be established, begin with the title. Use Anonymous only if that is the credited name.
  • APA uses (n.d.) when no date is available. MLA omits a missing publication date; an access date can help for undated or changing content. Do not infer a publication date from a search result or unrelated copyright year.
  • If there is no title, describe the work: APA uses a bracketed description; MLA uses a description without quotation marks or italics.
  • For an APA quotation without pages, use an appropriate paragraph, section, or timestamp. MLA uses source-provided numbering or timestamps when available; do not invent pages or count paragraphs for MLA.
  • Locate the original image or post. A search engine is not its creator. Verify the actual source before formatting an entry.
Before you submit
  • Use one citation style consistently and check that every in-text citation has a matching source-list entry.
  • Begin the final list on a new page. APA: center and bold References. MLA: center Works Cited in regular type.
  • Alphabetize entries, double-space, and apply a 0.5-inch hanging indent in your document’s paragraph settings. Do not number the entries.
  • Check actual names, dates, titles, source versions, and direct URLs. Reapply italics and hanging indents if copying loses formatting.
What about Chicago?

Chicago is another major citation style, with notes-and-bibliography and author-date systems. For this assignment, choose APA or MLA.
