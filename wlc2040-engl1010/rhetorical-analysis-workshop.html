From text to outline.

Share your text. Test your ideas. Plan your analysis.

Start when everyone is ready.

Three students = three rounds total, one 3-minute turn each. Groups of two can skip Speaker 3; groups of four can reset a sharing round for Speaker 4. Timers pause at zero; advance when the class is ready.

Concept reminders

Rhetorical situation: creator, audience, purpose, context, medium, and constraints. Exigence: the problem or need prompting the text. Logos: reasoning and evidence. Ethos: credibility and trust. Pathos: emotion and values. Explain how an appeal works for a particular audience; naming it is only the beginning.
