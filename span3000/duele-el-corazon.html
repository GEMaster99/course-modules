04 · Doler: information and an anticipated endpoint.

Same verb. Different construction.

Compare a current assertion with an anticipated endpoint after hasta que. A completed past endpoint can take indicative, as in Ayer corrí hasta que me dolieron las piernas.
INFORMATION PRESENTED AS CURRENT

Ahora me duelen las piernas.

The speaker states that they are in pain.

AN ENDPOINT STILL AHEAD

Voy a correr hasta que me duelan las piernas.

The pain marks a point the speaker has not reached yet.

These original examples reproduce the grammatical contrast in the chorus. Which words, besides the verb, explain the change?

Does hasta que always take subjunctive?

No. Compare a past event: Ayer corrí hasta que me dolieron las piernas. The endpoint is presented as reached. The future example uses subjunctive because its endpoint is still pending.

The key idea

Changing an ending is not enough. Look at the whole sentence.

05 · Subjunctive needs context.

Why not simply “mañana hablemos”?

Mañana hablemos on its own normally works as “Let’s talk tomorrow.” It does not simply make the plan less certain. To express hope or possibility, add the relevant construction: espero que or puede que.

Choose what you want to express. Notice what changes besides the ending.

“But I am sure we will talk…”

You can be sure and still say Cuando hablemos mañana, decidimos. Personal certainty does not change this use of temporal cuando. The event is still pending.

Mañana hablamos presents a plan; it does not guarantee that the plan will happen.

Does subjunctive mean “unreal”?

No. A reaction can take subjunctive even when the situation is real. Future plans and open si conditions can use indicative. Check both the meaning and the surrounding construction.

07 · A bridge to commands.

One form, more than one job

Negative tú commands use present subjunctive forms. Affirmative tú commands have their own forms. Usted and ustedes commands use subjunctive forms in both affirmative and negative commands.

A hope

Espero que hables con ella.

I express what I hope for.

A negative command

No hables por ella.

I tell someone what not to do.

Compare Te recomiendo que pienses, Piensa, and No pienses. Does the intention change, the structure, or both?

08 · Give advice without deciding for someone else.

Now speak as their friend

Give advice as the listener’s friend. Include information, a recommendation, and either an open condition or a future time clause. Your notes save on this browser only and are not submitted to Dr. Rama.

The person in the song asks you for advice. Focus on their perspective and wishes. Prepare brief notes, then speak without reading a script.

Exit question

Explain in your own words: why is “it has not happened yet” not enough to choose subjunctive?

Check the central idea

Because we also use indicative for future plans and open conditions. Identify both the meaning and the construction: mañana hablamos; si vienes; cuando vengas; espero que vengas.
