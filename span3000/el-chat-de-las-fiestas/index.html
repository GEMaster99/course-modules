Choose the time frame, then the mood

  1. Time frame. Use the presente perfecto for an action already completed by now or by a relevant future point. Use the pluscuamperfecto for an action that happened before another point in the past.
  2. Mood. Use the indicative when the speaker presents the event as known, certain, or believed. Use the subjunctive in contexts such as doubt, hope, and reactions. A reaction can take the subjunctive even when the event really happened.
  3. Form. Conjugate haber for the subject of the blank and add the past participle. With haber, the participle stays the same.

Participles and pronoun placement

hablar → hablado · comer → comido · vivir → vivido

abrir → abierto · decir → dicho · escribir → escrito · hacer → hecho · poner → puesto · resolver → resuelto · ver → visto · volver → vuelto

Keep any reflexive or object pronoun before haber. Compare se han preparado and se habían preparado.

Common mistakes · Can you fix the chat?

For each message, try correcting the highlighted phrase before revealing the fix.

  1. Dudo que tú has envuelto mi sudadera.

    Reveal the fix

    hayas envuelto. Dudo que calls for the subjunctive. The speaker is doubting now whether the wrapping has already happened.

  2. Me alegra que ustedes han traído postre.

    Reveal the fix

    hayan traído. A reaction such as me alegra que takes the subjunctive even when everyone can see the dessert.

  3. Ayer, cuando empezó el intercambio, sabía que Ana ya ha abierto el regalo.

    Reveal the fix

    había abierto. The gift was opened before the exchange began yesterday. Sabía que presents that earlier event as known.

  4. Durante la cena, dudaba que ellos hayan puesto el pastel en la nevera antes de que llegáramos.

    Reveal the fix

    hubieran puesto. The doubt is anchored in the past, and the action would have happened before we arrived. Hubiesen puesto also works.

  5. Espero que ustedes haya hacido sitio para mi amiga.

    Reveal the fix

    hayan hecho. Match haber to ustedes. The past participle of hacer is hecho.

  6. Sé que ellos han preparados los regalos.

    Reveal the fix

    han preparado. With haber, only the auxiliary changes for the subject. The participle stays preparado.

  7. Me sorprende que el perro haya comídose el panecillo.

    Reveal the fix

    se haya comido. The reflexive pronoun goes before the conjugated form of haber.
