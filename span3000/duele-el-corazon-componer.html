Same situation. Two ways to present it.

A · Open possibility · Song

Si te vas,
yo también me voy.

“If you leave, I leave too.”

The singer treats leaving as an open condition, without guaranteeing it will happen.

Si + present indicative → present, future, or command result.

B · Imagined situation · Our rewrite

Si te fueras,
yo también me iría.

“If you were to leave, I would leave too.”

The speaker presents a more remote or imagined scenario. It need not be impossible.

Si + imperfect subjunctive → conditional result. Me iría is conditional, not subjunctive.

Discuss: Could both be about tonight? What changes besides the ending after si?

Both can be future. For an open condition use si vienes, not si vengas. For an imagined one: si vinieras, bailaríamos.

They are all about tomorrow.

Why do the forms differ? Explain which construction licenses each highlighted form.

Original exampleReason
Mañana hablamos.A plan presented with indicative.
Si vienes mañana, hablamos.An open condition with conditional si.
Cuando vengas mañana, hablamos.An anticipated event in a temporal cuando clause.
Espero que mañana hablemos.A hope expressed with espero que.
C · Add present subjunctive with a reason. The song contrasts te duelen los pies with hasta que duelan los pies: a description versus an anticipated endpoint.
Can I just say “mañana hablemos” if I am less sure?

By itself, it usually means “Let’s talk tomorrow.” For hope, use Espero que mañana hablemos. For an anticipated time clause, use Cuando hablemos mañana, decidimos. Future time by itself does not select subjunctive.

Discuss the choice. Then check.

1 · An invitation or a more remote imagined scene?

Si vuelves, te guardo un lugar.
Si volvieras, te guardaría un lugar.

Which treats returning as open? Could both refer to tonight? How might each sound in a song?

The first leaves returning open. The second imagines a more remote scenario. Both may refer to tonight. Notice that vuelves → volvieras and guardo → guardaría both change.

2 · Repair a line for an open possibility

Si vengas, bailamos.

What would you change? Then say the whole idea as an imagined situation.

Si vienes, bailamos. Conditional si uses present indicative here. For the imagined version: Si vinieras, bailaríamos. Changing to subjunctive also changes how the situation is presented.

3 · Does subjunctive mean the situation is unreal?

No me importa que estés aquí.
Sé que estás aquí.

Could the person actually be here in both? Which expression explains each verb form?

Yes. No me importa que introduces a reaction with subjunctive here. Sé que presents a claim using indicative. A reaction can concern something real.

Need a starting structure or a verb form?

A: Si tú ___, yo ___.
B: Si tú ___, yo ___-ía.
C: Espero que tú ___. / Quiero que tú ___. / Bailaremos hasta que ___.

Imperfect subjunctive: preterite ellos form minus -ron + -ra, -ras, -ra, -ramos, -rais, -ran. For tú: vinieras, te fueras, tuvieras, pudieras. Nosotros needs an accent: viniéramos.

Conditional: infinitive + -ía, -ías, -ía, -íamos, -íais, -ían. Examples: bailaría, bailaríamos, me iría, tendría, podría. Use the form for your chosen subject. Alternative imperfect-subjunctive -se forms are also valid.

Use the structures to express your own idea, rather than copying a complete model.
