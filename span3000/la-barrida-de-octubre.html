Notas del docenteRegional variation to elicit from heritage speakers: lanzar (widespread) vs. tirar (Mexico) vs. pitchear (informal, Caribbean); conectar / pegar / batear; botarla (Caribbean) vs. volarse la barda (Mexico); salvamento (Mexico) vs. rescate (Caribbean); cachar (Mexico, colloquial); abanicar for a swinging strikeout (Mexico); roletazo (Mexico) vs. rolling (Caribbean) for a grounder. Usage varies by country and announcer, so ask students which forms they grew up hearing before presenting any as the standard. Game examples (Turang HR, Lara RBI single, Misiorowski start, Megill save, Hall win and Snell loss in Game 3, Chourio's last out) come from the recaps and box scores checked on Oct 16, 2026; the other examples are generic.

Notas del docenteRegional variants for "I'm a fan of": le voy a (Mexico), soy hincha de (Southern Cone, Spain), soy aficionado/fanático de (widespread). Chaquetero is common in Mexico and the Caribbean. Hinchada is more usual for soccer than for baseball.

Béisbol en la vida diaria

Notas del docenteDar la vuelta a la tortilla is widespread; voltear la tortilla / voltearse la tortilla is the more Latin American form. Invite students to produce one sentence about their own life with each expression.
